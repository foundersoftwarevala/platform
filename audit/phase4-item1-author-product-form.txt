The Product Demo Manager's AddProduct.tsx is a developer-only operator page,
and MarketplaceCatalogAdmin.tsx is the operator catalogue editor. Neither is
the author-facing product form. The actual author dashboard uses
AuthorProductsWorkspace. That form exposed the supported field groups, but
client validation did not mirror the API's minimum name length and HTTP(S)
URL checks.
