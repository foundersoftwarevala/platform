Root cause:
- The existing Media Library called the real `/api/marketplace/media` endpoint
  for metadata and signed downloads, but did not render storage images.
- The Product Media section used the generic `media_library` resource shell
  instead of the marketplace media endpoint.

  without backend/schema support.
